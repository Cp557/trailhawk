Trailhawk

A tri-tiltrotor VTOL drone that takes off straight up, flies on its wing and circles over a hiker on high trails, scanning for wildlife with thermal and color cameras. Sized for 12,000 ft, it comes apart to fit a backpack. Modeled in Python with build123d from real, buyable parts.
